How to export, and import settings

In DeepCharts: Trading → Strategy Report, pick your account and dates, Generate Report, open the Trade List tab, then Export CSV. Re-importing overlapping dates is fine: duplicates are skipped.

The export has no stop prices, so imported trades get the placeholder stop and are marked for review. Edit each one to set the setup, real stop and whether you followed your rules. MFE and MAE come from DeepCharts' Run-up and DrawDown, which only cover the time you were in the trade.
